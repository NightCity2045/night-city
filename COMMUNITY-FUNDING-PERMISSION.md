## 1. Voluntary donations

A noncommercial project using the covered software may request and accept
genuinely voluntary donations.

A donation is genuinely voluntary only when:

1. payment is not required to access, use, download, play, or participate in
   the project;

2. the donor receives no product, service, access, privilege, priority,
   advantage, authority, influence, or other consideration in exchange;

3. the amount of the payment does not determine the donor's rights,
   capabilities, access, or treatment within the project; and

4. the payment is not part of a sale, subscription, sponsorship,
   advertisement, paid promotion, or other commercial arrangement.

## 2. Hosting and infrastructure funding

Players and community members may:

1. contribute money toward reasonable hosting and infrastructure expenses;

2. pay a hosting or infrastructure provider directly;

3. reimburse documented hosting and infrastructure expenses;

4. contribute to a reasonable reserve intended for future hosting and
   infrastructure expenses.

Permitted infrastructure expenses include:

* physical or virtual server hosting;
* network services;
* domain registration;
* DDoS protection;
* backups and storage;
* monitoring services;
* databases and other technical infrastructure;
* transaction fees associated with receiving permitted funding.

Using an ordinary commercial hosting provider does not by itself make the
project's use of the covered software commercial.

## 3. Donor acknowledgment

A project may publish a donor's name or username as a simple acknowledgment.

A symbolic donor label, badge, or role is permitted only when it provides no:

* access;
* permissions;
* authority;
* queue priority;
* reserved slot;
* gameplay advantage;
* item or currency;
* cosmetic content;
* voting power;
* administrative influence;
* additional functionality; or
* other practical benefit.

The acknowledgment must not function as advertising or commercial
sponsorship.

## 4. Activities not permitted

This additional permission does not permit:

1. paid access, subscriptions, memberships, or paid whitelist access;

2. reserved slots or queue priority in exchange for payment;

3. virtual items, currency, equipment, characters, professions, abilities,
   progression, cosmetics, or other content in exchange for payment;

4. administrative or moderation authority in exchange for payment;

5. advertising, sponsorships, affiliate marketing, product placement, or
   paid promotion;

6. the sale, rental, or commercial licensing of the covered software;

7. paid development, administration, consulting, hosting, support, or other
   services based on the covered software;

8. payments that are described as donations but are actually connected to a
   product, service, privilege, advantage, or commercial arrangement; or

9. arrangements intended to disguise commercial use as community funding.
